Version 2.1 (Navigation, Tiles, Contact Form & Ticker)
-----------------------------------------------------
1. Hover dropdown menus on ALL 7 tabs (Home, About Us, Our Products, Achievements & Media,
   Gallery, Careers, Contact Us), modelled on the Our Products mega-menu. Hover-intent on
   desktop, keyboard support (Tab / ArrowDown / Escape), tap-to-open on touch, accordion on mobile.
   Header now switches to the hamburger layout below 1200px so the tabs and CTA always fit.
   Small menu thumbnails live in assets/images/nav/.
2. Uniform tiles: Achievements, Media and Gallery now use equal-sized tiles (4:3 image frame,
   fixed caption area). Posters/certificates are shown whole over a blurred backdrop; gallery
   photos are cover-cropped. A lone last tile is centred. Full image/caption opens in the lightbox.
3. Contact page: new "Send Us a Message" form (name, email, phone, organisation, subject, message)
   with inline validation and a honeypot. Posts to https://medzak.co/esend.php using the same
   field names as the Book-a-Demo form; subject is prefixed to userComments.
4. Home page: LinkedIn motion bar now sits directly under the hero, in the reference style
   (dark band, "Innovating for disease prevention" banner, LinkedIn icons, pause on hover).
5. Anchors added: about-us.html#mission/#vision/#solution, index.html#news/#supporters,
   achievements.html#supporters, contact.html#contact-form/#map.
