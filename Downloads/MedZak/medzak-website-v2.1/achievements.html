Achievements

2026 She Cohort 4.0Our CEO was selected as one of the 15 new members of SHE Cohort 4.0, Punjab State Council for Science and Technology, 2026.
TiE 2025Medzak won TiE Women 2025-Chandigarh Chapter. Our CEO further represented the Chandigarh Chapter at the TiE Women 2026 Global Pitch Competition, TiE Global Summit, 2026.
SM 2024Medzak Healthcare Pvt. Ltd. won the prestigious Startup Maha Rathi Challenge in the Health and Biotech sector, a highlight of India’s innovation event, Startup Mahakumbh 2025, held at Bharat Mandapam, New Delhi.
BT 2020Our CEO was the winner of the 3rd Edition of the BIOTECH WInER Award (Women Entrepreneurial Research), conferred by BIRAC-TiE Delhi, NCR, 2020.
Falling Walls 2019Dr. Varsha Singh was selected as one of the entrepreneurs to present at Falling Walls Lab India 2019, Manipal, organized by the German Academic Exchange Service (DAAD), the German Center for Research and Innovation (DWIH), New Delhi.
BIRAC_TiE 2020Medzak Healthcare was one of the top winners of the TiE-BIRAC WInER awards for women in entrepreneurial research, in the fields of science, technology, biotech, and pharma, 2020.
Matlab 2020Dr. Varsha Singh had the privilege of sharing thoughts on Women in Tech, the need for gender parity, and her journey toward entrepreneurship at MATLAB Expo 2020.
IITM 2020Team Medzak as the winner of the Med-Tech Virtual-a-thon conducted by IITM HTIC MedTech Incubator, 2020.
FAOBMB 2019Dr. Varsha Singh was awarded the Young Scientist Travel Award by UNESCO to present her research & innovation at the FAOBMB 2019, Kuala Lumpur, Malaysia, 2019.
MedTech 2019Medzak Healthcare stood second place in the MedTech Challenge’19, organised by Entrepreneurship Cell, VIT & VIT-Technology Business Incubator, at Vellore Institute of Technology, Vellore, 2019.
Thub 2017Dr. Varsha Singh was a Top 10 innovator in the Intel & DST-Innovate for Digital India Challenge 2.0 at T-HUB, Hyderabad.
BRICS 2017Dr. Varsha Singh was selected as a Young Scientist representing India at the 2nd BRICS Young Scientist Conclave in collaboration with the Department of Science & Technology, India, and the Ministry of Science & Technology, China, held at Hangzhou, China, 2017.
