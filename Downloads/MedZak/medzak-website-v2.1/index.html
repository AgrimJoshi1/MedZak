Metabo-X

AI-powered personalized metabolomics analysis

Detect disease risk years before symptoms appear. Metabo-X analyzes disease-linked metabolites from a simple blood sample, using AI to generate a personalized risk score, nutrition guidance, and physician-reviewed therapy recommendations — built as a clinical decision aid, not a replacement for diagnosis.

  • Analyzes disease-linked metabolites from a simple blood sample
  • AI-generated personalized risk score
  • Nutrition guidance

About Us

Founded in 2018, Medzak Healthcare started with a mission to close the gap between the world’s growing burden of eye and metabolic disease and the diagnostic tools available to detect it early — before it becomes expensive, invasive, or irreversible.

Why Medzak

  • Patented optical technology
  • Clinically validated AI
  • Portable, non-invasive diagnostics using surface sensing
  • Built for hospitals, labs, and outreach camps
Read our story

Our Mission

To build portable, affordable, AI-powered diagnostic devices for ocular and metabolic health, that bring hospital-grade screening to OPDs, outreach camps, and underserved communities.

Our Vision

To see a world where no patient loses their sight, or their health, to a disease that could have been caught early.

Our Solution

Across two platforms, MeiboX for ocular diagnostics and Metabo-X for metabolic risk prediction, we combine imaging, AI, and clinical validation into devices that are fast, low-cost, and built for real-world care.
